# B41 — Assessment Intelligence → Misconception & Remediation Graph

## Principle

Question-response patterns can identify a **likely weak concept**, but they do not prove a learner's psychological misconception. Student OS therefore uses the term **concept signal** and routes that signal through canonical prerequisite relationships.

## Decision chain

`question responses → concept signal → prerequisite check → smallest remediation path → fresh verification`

## Safety of the model

- No diagnosis from one wrong answer.
- Repeated misses must span distinct questions before becoming a strong signal.
- Canonical prerequisite links outrank a broad target-topic recommendation when the prerequisite is weak.
- Remediation planning is read-only and does not silently write mastery, sessions, tasks, or evidence.
- Verification uses fresh assessment evidence rather than reusing the same attempt.
