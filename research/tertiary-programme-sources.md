# Tertiary Programme Selection Sources

The onboarding programme catalogue is a **curated generic selection aid**, not an assertion that any particular institution offers every listed programme. The University of Ghana’s official programme catalogue and undergraduate admissions pages were consulted to verify that undergraduate offerings span diverse BA, BSc, health, professional, and engineering pathways rather than a school-subject list.

| Source                                       | URL                                                 | Use in Student OS                                             |
| -------------------------------------------- | --------------------------------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------- |
| University of Ghana Programme Catalogue      | https://www.ug.edu.gh/programme-catalogue           | Representative official degree-programme catalogue reference. |
| University of Ghana Undergraduate Admissions | https://admissions.ug.edu.gh/undergraduate/overview | Representative undergraduate degree/pathway reference.        |
| University of Ghana Undergraduate Study      | https://www.ug.edu.gh/academics/undergraduate-study | Confirms a diverse, institution-specific programme landscape. |

Learners can always add their exact university programme. Institution-specific official-course verification needs the learner’s institution and is not inferred or misrepresented as a global catalogue.
