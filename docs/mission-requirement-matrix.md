## Explicit unresolved areas

The most important remaining source-audit areas are the command-center/today-plan prioritization contract, full exam-to-learning end-to-end evidence, mastery and recommendation completeness, cross-collection search, account isolation and offline hydration proofs, and accessibility/mobile/performance acceptance. The server-authoritative assessment session/submission system and provenance-aware country/education-system catalogue remain deferred architectural work. Neither should replace the local practice runner or invalidate custom subjects.

## Evidence discipline

The matrix distinguishes source proof from runtime acceptance. No claim here substitutes for testing real OAuth providers, push delivery, browser permissions, installed-PWA updates, storage/PDF providers, physical devices, screen readers, keyboard/touch/zoom interaction, timezone transitions, low-end performance, offline recovery, or live multi-device conflict.
