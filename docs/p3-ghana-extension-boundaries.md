# P3 Ghana-first extension boundaries

Student OS may add a **Ghana-first academic-content discovery layer** only as an opt-in enhancement to the learner’s private workspace. The Ministry of Education curriculum resource hub identifies itself as a resource platform for SHS, SHTS, STEM, basic, JHS, and TVET learners, with learner and teacher pathways.[1] The National Council for Curriculum and Assessment describes its role in developing learner-focused national curriculum and standardising/monitoring assessment, and exposes curriculum and assessment resources.[2]

| Future capability            | Boundary required before release                                                                                         | Current P3 decision                                     |
| ---------------------------- | ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------- |
| Curriculum alignment         | Link to official sources; do not copy or claim ownership of curriculum material; disclose source and last-checked date.  | Prepared as a source-governed future integration.       |
| Ghana-first topic mapping    | Begin with learner-selected level and subject, then map only to curated official resource links.                         | No automatic scraping or unreviewed generated syllabus. |
| Peer sharing or class spaces | Invite-only groups, server-enforced membership, reporting, moderation, and age-appropriate safeguards are prerequisites. | Not released.                                           |
| Advanced analytics           | Opt-in, explainable, account-scoped summaries; no behavioural advertising or sale of learner data.                       | Not released.                                           |
| Cross-device features        | Reuse the established account-scoped local-first workspace and conflict-safe sync layer.                                 | Already supported by the core architecture.             |

The P3 roadmap deliberately does **not** create a public feed, open leaderboard, or unmoderated social network. Any future sharing capability must begin with private, consent-led groups and enforce ownership and membership on the server.
