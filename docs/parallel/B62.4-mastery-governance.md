# B62.4 — Deterministic mastery governance and reassessment foundation

## Source checkpoint and isolation

Branch `parallel/b62-4-mastery-governance` starts at verified B62.3
`1d11a489c9554ef4ade2ed618e2fe2fc0e748c9f` (draft PR #9).
B62.1–B62.3 remain separate, earlier release gates intact, and `main`
unchanged.

## The distinction that must remain explicit

`client/src/lib/learningIntelligence.ts` is the existing deterministic,
learner-local **mastery estimate** for advice, recovery, and scheduling.
It does not establish official independent assessment.

B62.3's account-protected, read-only `assessments.evidenceReview` checks
server-scored learner-origin quiz snapshots, answers, and submission
integrity. It deliberately returns `independence: "unknown"`,
`sourceApproval: "unverified"`, and
`authoritativeMasteryEligible: false` for **every** case. A server-
finalized score is neither an independent exam nor a source-reviewed
curriculum assessment.

A client-supplied policy object, even one with `published` and
`approvedBy` fields, is not evidence of a trusted institutional
authority, registered curriculum or valid authorization.

## B62.4 read-only contract

`shared/masteryReadiness.ts` defines a bounded, strict and
non-authoritative review format. It can represent:
- `insufficient_evidence`
- `estimated` (advisory scores only)
- `needs_review`

It **cannot represent `verified`**; literal
`verifiedMastery: false` is required and validation fails on injected
approval flags. Every result carries canonical context/policy/version/
outcome references, bounded assessment session IDs, and explicit unresolved
policy, source, context-to-assessment, and independence gates.

`server/masteryReadiness.ts` provides a deterministic pure evaluator and
reassessment comparison over **server-supplied** academic context,
policy metadata, account-owner scope, and B62.3 review facts.

- An owner mismatch blocks estimation and suppresses evidence IDs.
- Context changes, missing or mismatched policies, duplicate session IDs,
  different outcome topics, compromised/questionable submissions, and
  future timestamps hold the evaluation for review.
- Only structurally intact, finalized, same-outcome learner-workspace
  assessment reviews contribute to the *advisory* mean score.
- Reassessment recomputes from the complete evidence snapshot and
  distinguishes new evidence, evidence removal, changed review state,
  changed academic scope, and unchanged state.
- The calculation never mutates the workspace, assessment submission,
  ledger, or existing `learningIntelligence` output.

There is **no new public endpoint**. `MasteryReadinessFacts` must
never be wired to a client-submitted policy, authenticated subject, or
evidence object. Such a route would allow forged governance and
cross-account references. Integration requires a separately designed
trusted owner-bound service.

## Prerequisites for true authoritative mastery and durable records

Before adding a MySQL decision ledger, the production design must
provide all of the following:

1. **Authoritative academic context registry:** immutable versioned JHS,
   SHS and tertiary scoped mappings with authenticated account ownership;
   historical, remedial and private-candidate status as context, not
   automatically approved policy. Institutional programme versions and
   transitions cannot be inferred from display labels.
2. **Institutional/academic authority registry:** independently verified
   publisher/approver identities, permitted jurisdictions and subjects,
   reviewed source lineage, version/revocation records, protected policy
   publication, and explicit signatures or trusted approval events.
   A self-described `approvedBy` value is not sufficient.
3. **Assessment provenance:** protected registered assessment source,
   immutable delivery and scoring evidence, independence/exam conditions,
   human-review provenance when required, anti-replay and account binding.
   B62.3 learner-origin quizzes remain advisory even at 100%.
4. **Level-specific approved mastery rubric:** explicit minimum evidence,
   thresholds, prerequisite requirements, freshness/reassessment and
   moderation rules, versioned and sourced. Do not invent universal
   Ghana-wide grades or thresholds from a generic quiz percentage.
5. **Append-only decision ledger:** additive proposal for MySQL tables
   `academic_context_versions`, `academic_policy_approvals`,
   `evidence_decision_events`, and `mastery_decision_events` with
   owner-scope and unique idempotency event keys, predecessor linkage,
   transactionally stable evidence and policy version references,
   decision timestamps, actor/role, reason codes, audit trail and explicit
   supersession/revocation. No destructive rewrite of existing tables,
   materialized client-claimed decision status, or offline auto-promotion.
6. **Authorization and verification:** server-only writes through
   reviewed RBAC/ABAC controls and account ownership checks, robust
   transaction/replay tests, offline merge conflict reconciliation,
   separate official-source review, and live authenticated API E2E
   validation after B58.1 `DATABASE_URL`/OAuth fixes.

No database schema/migrations, external source approval, institution
assertions, NEXA-based awards, production env writes, release gate
bypasses, or merges are authorized by this milestone.

## B62.4 checks

Regression tests cover all JHS/SHS/tertiary contexts, private-candidate
context, perfect-score non-promotion, absent/mismatched published policy,
invalid context, owner mismatch, integrity holds, duplicate/replayed
submissions, outcome mismatch, future timestamps, deterministic ordering,
forged verified claims, missing evidence, evidence addition/revocation,
review-state regression, and academic scope transitions.

CI must independently pass locked pnpm install, TypeScript app/tests/
config, ESLint, Prettier, full Vitest, bundle/build, clean CI MySQL
migrations, credential scan, and full/production dependency audits.
Only those actually observed passing can be claimed.

Even with green CI, **B62.4 is not finished as a production
authoritative mastery system.** This foundation is a verified
non-authoritative safeguard and blueprint until official authorization,
durable decision persistence and live E2E checks are available.
